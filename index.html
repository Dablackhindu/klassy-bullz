About us

A hobby kennel, on purpose

Klassy Bullz is a small, home-based kennel — one or two litters a year, every puppy whelped and raised in our living room. We sit below the licensing threshold because staying small is the point: we know every puppy's personality by name, and every dog we breed from is DNA-tested for the full breed panel first.

We breed French Bulldogs because we live with them, and we breed for the things that matter in this breed: open airways, sound structure, and temperaments that make them the family clowns they're meant to be.

You'll always visit or video call before anything is agreed, you'll always meet mum, and you'll collect your puppy in person — or we'll deliver personally once you've met. We never courier puppies, full stop.

The Klassy Bullz pledge

  • Full breed DNA panel on every sire and dam — results shared before you commit
  • Visit-first, always: meet mum and the litter before any money changes hands
  • No couriered puppies — collection in person, or personal delivery after you've met
  • Lifetime breeder support, and we take our dogs back at any age, no questions
  • Health-first pairings — airways and structure before colour, every time
